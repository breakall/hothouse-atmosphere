The moves that matter

Catch. Smear. Play over it.

Left = wet effects

Tap engages or bypasses the wet section. Hold ≥ 0.4 s freezes recent wet output while the section is on; release to resume. Solid LED = on. Blinking = frozen.

Right = micro-loop

The buffer is always listening. Tap catches what you just played; tap again to replace it with new audio. Hold ≥ 0.4 s adds dry input to an existing loop. Solid LED = playing; blinking = overdub; dim pulse = listening.

Start clean

On power-up both sections are bypassed. Loops disappear at power-off. Taps trigger on release. Both switches held ≥ 2 s enter USB flashing mode, rather than a sound-editing menu.

Rates are internal; audio I/O stays at 48 kHz. Times are the maximum listening history after the buffer fills. Turning CLOCK down after capture lowers pitch and extends playback; turning it down before capture gives a longer recording window. Step boundaries have hysteresis. Overdubbing adds only dry input.

About these recipes. Based on the techniques described in the official MOOD MkII manual (wet modes pp. 22–27; looping pp. 28–35; routing pp. 40–41). Knob settings and names here are original Atmosphere adaptations. Atmosphere uses its own algorithms; expect related textures rather than identical sounds. In particular, frozen wet output is a recorded segment: MODIFY does not reshape it while frozen. No MIDI, presets, ramping, selectable spread or sync in v0.1. True stereo is supported; mono input is not automatically duplicated. Physical numbering follows Cleveland Music Co.’s Hothouse layout. Offline, self-contained guide · no connection to the pedal.
